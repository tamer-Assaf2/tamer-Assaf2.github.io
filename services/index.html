A clear path from problem to handover.

The process scales to the engagement without hiding risks or promising fixed outcomes before the scope is understood.

  1. DiscoverClarify users, workflows, data, constraints, and the expected operational result.
  2. ScopeDefine responsibilities, milestones, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and privacy boundaries.
  3. Build and validateDeliver in reviewable increments with testing, documentation, and visible decisions.
  4. HandoverProvide deployment guidance, knowledge transfer, and an agreed support path.
