One platform from cart to branch operations.

The platform combines a customer storefront with the operational systems required to manage products, branches, warehouses, orders, picking, delivery, point of sale, returns, invoices, and loyalty.

Web and Android experiences use the same application services while role-based access, branch scope, audit records, and transaction rules protect operational consistency.

Backend and database

Commerce operations

Implemented product, inventory, order, fulfillment, payment, invoice, promotion, loyalty, return, and reporting workflows.

Branch isolation

Key engineering decisions.

Transactional inventory

Reservation, release, and final deduction rules keep stock changes explicit and auditable through the order lifecycle.

Idempotent payments

Payment callbacks and order activation are designed to handle retries without duplicating financial actions.

Role and branch control

A configurable permission matrix combines role capabilities with branch-scoped employee access.

Modular delivery

A modular monolith keeps business domains separated while retaining straightforward deployment and transaction management.

Advanced development status.

The web store, administration surfaces, and Android application operate end to end. Payment and OTP workflows have been verified, with production readiness work continuing before launch.
